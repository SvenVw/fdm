Added the Atlas map layer "Bemestingsadvies". It colours every field of a farm by the nutrient advice for the selected nutrient (in kg/ha): still to apply, percentage of the advice, or the advice itself. Hovering a field shows the advice, the amount already applied and the amount still to apply; clicking a field opens its fertilization advice page. The per-field advice calculation is now shared with the Bemestingsadvies overview.
