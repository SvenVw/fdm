Added the option `useEffectiveNitrogen` to the nitrogen balance to calculate the nitrogen supply by fertilizers with effective nitrogen (`p_n_rt * p_n_wc`) instead of total nitrogen. The default remains total nitrogen. In the app, the nitrogen balance pages have a switch to show the balance with effective nitrogen.
