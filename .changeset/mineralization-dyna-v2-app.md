Redesign the Mineralisatie app (Labs) around Dyna v2. The farm overview shows per field the available nitrogen, uptake and leaching, and lists the fields that need attention: a missing harvest, an expected nitrogen shortage, rain around a planned application or harvest, or a nitrogen surplus. The field page shows an interactive chart of nitrogen supply, uptake, available nitrogen and nitrate leaching with sowing, fertilization and harvest events, the weather forecast, the workable nitrogen balance, the data used for the calculation, and a what-if scenario to shift or change applications and the harvest before saving them. Planning, growing season and evaluation are pre-selected based on the date and can be switched. When the harvest is missing, the page asks for the measured or planned harvest. The pages for soil nitrogen supply (MINIP, PMN, Century) are removed. Calculations stream in per field, so the page no longer waits for all fields.
